O-GlcNAc biology

O-linked β-N-acetylglucosamine is a dynamic post-translational modification on serine, threonine, and tyrosine residues. It is regulated by OGT and OGA and has been implicated in transcription, translation, cell-cycle control, metabolism, and signaling.

Predictions support experimental prioritization; they are not experimental evidence. Each resource retains its own evidence and interpretation criteria.
