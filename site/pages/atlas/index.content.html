About the resource

Experimentally identified sites across species

Protein O-GlcNAcylation has been documented in all metazoans (including animals, insects, and plants), some bacteria, fungi, and viruses. O-GlcNAcylation occurs on many proteins in the nucleus, cytosol, and mitochondria. As a critical modification, O-GlcNAc functions in a species-, tissue-/cell-, protein-, and site-specific manner.
