How it works

Classify HexNAc spectra from five diagnostic ions

Upload a CSV containing oxonium-ion intensities at m/z 126, 138, 144, 168, and 186. HexNAcQuest normalizes each spectrum and classifies it as GlcNAc or GalNAc using the published logistic regression model.
