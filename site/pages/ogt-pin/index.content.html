Why OGT-PIN?

Interaction context for OGT biology

Interactions between proteins are essential to cellular processes and form the molecular networks that determine a cell’s functional state. Defining the interactome of OGT, the sole enzyme that adds O-GlcNAc to its target proteins, can facilitate studies of OGT biology, protein O-GlcNAcylation, protein/pathway/process function, drug development, and related areas.

OGT Protein Interaction Network (OGT-PIN) is a curated and comprehensive database of experimentally identified interaction proteins of OGT and its orthologues in multiple species studied over the past several decades.
