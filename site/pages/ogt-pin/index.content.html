Why OGT-PIN?

Interaction context for OGT biology

OGT-PIN curates experimentally identified interaction proteins of OGT and its orthologues across species. Searchable identifiers, methods, publications, and curator assessments support studies of OGT networks and O-GlcNAc biology.
